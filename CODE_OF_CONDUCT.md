# HyperParallel Code of Conduct

**English** | [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh.md)

HyperParallel adopts the [LF Projects Code of Conduct](https://lfprojects.org/policies/code-of-conduct/).
Everyone who uses, contributes to, or maintains HyperParallel is welcome to participate on equal terms.
The code applies in issues, pull requests, project chats and meetings, and when someone publicly represents HyperParallel.

## Working together

- Welcome questions from people at every level of experience. Give credit to the people whose work you build on.
- In a review, explain the concern and give the author a way to investigate it. For example, if a patch slows down training,
  share the benchmark, hardware and configuration so others can reproduce the result.
- Respect people's privacy. Get permission before sharing private messages or personal details.
- Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks and unwelcome sexual attention are unacceptable.
  This includes private messages connected to project activities and retaliation against anyone who raises a concern.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness misconduct, you can email a maintainer privately.
The [Parallel Training System SIG directory](sig-info.yaml)
lists the maintainers and their email addresses. Choose someone uninvolved in the incident.

Share what happened, where it happened, and any links or excerpts you feel comfortable providing.
You do not need to open a public issue or contact the person involved.

## How maintainers respond

An uninvolved maintainer reviews the report and shares details only with those who need them to address it.
Depending on the seriousness of the conduct and whether it has happened before, maintainers may ask someone to change
their behavior, remove harmful content, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently.
If you believe a decision missed important context, you can ask another uninvolved maintainer to review it privately.
